# AI client icons

These icons identify the AI clients that Tsumu MCP supports on the connection
guide (`/`) and in the Tsumu editor's MCP connection dialog. They come from
[`@lobehub/icons-static-svg`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg)
(MIT) with only the size attributes normalized.

The marks themselves are trademarks of their respective owners (OpenAI,
Anthropic, Anysphere, Google). They are used only to refer to those products;
Tsumu is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
